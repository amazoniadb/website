# Sources to verify

Real institutions relevant to AmazoniaDB's domains that remain to be reviewed for `data/catalog.js`. These are leads, not verified entries — each one still needs someone to confirm the exact URL, read the actual access/reuse terms (don't guess), and write a plain-language description, per `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

Pick an unchecked item, verify it, submit it through the issue form or a pull request like any other entry. Check the box once it's merged into the catalog.

The working catalog now has 100 records: 31 Brazil, 16 global-subsettable, 10 pan-Amazon, 8 Colombia, 6 each for Bolivia, Ecuador, and Peru, 5 each for Guyana and Venezuela, 4 Suriname, and 3 French Guiana. The first multi-country expansion is indexed; the unchecked items below are the remaining discovery queue, not approved sources.

## Also open

Search each national portal in the local language and keep a lead unchecked until its direct page, access terms, coverage, methods, and relevance have been reviewed.
