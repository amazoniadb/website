# Security policy

Please report vulnerabilities privately through [GitHub's private advisory form](https://github.com/amazoniadb/website/security/advisories/new) or by email to `correcoes@amazoniadb.org`.

Do not open a public issue when a report contains an exploitable vulnerability, credentials, personal information, or details that could put people, territories, communities, or species at risk. Include the affected URL or file, the impact, and a minimal reproduction when it is safe to do so.

The maintained scope is the current `main` branch and `https://amazoniadb.org/`. Third-party datasets and publisher websites linked from the directory remain under their publishers' control.
